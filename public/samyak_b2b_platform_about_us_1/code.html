A Custom Label Manufacturer with Experience & Expertise

Every Design has it’s Persona, far beyond colours & appearance. It includes Values, Culture, what Brand stands for and Emotions that will evoke customer Desire. Researches states that, Innovative packaging draws consumer attention and ensure competitive advantage of a Brand.

It is so important to ensure that the product is protected well enough to preserve it’s characteristics, extend it’s shelf life and encourage consumers to grab it. We, SIPL, are well equipped with innovative & unique design printing & packaging facility in our State-of-Art plant, to protect Brand of our valuable customers and to serve mainly Pharmaceutical, Food, Healthcare, Agro & entire FMCG sector by following SOP while maintaining Quality Standards.

Choosing a right Label, suitable Packaging material and perfect finishing is the wisest and most economical start to Product Brand building. We always keep ourselves updated with the latest trends in industries across the Globe and guide our customers in developing the right solution for their product.

We provide the solutions having combination of multiple printing options, print processes, suitable materials, perfect finishing, specialty inks, specially designed Die Punch sizes and security features which helps our customers to differentiate their product from others on a crowded shelf.
