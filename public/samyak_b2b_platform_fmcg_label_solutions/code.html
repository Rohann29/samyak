Types of Labels

Engineered solutions tailored to high-speed lines and consumer shelf impact.

Product Labels

High-quality primary packaging labels built for bottles, pouches, tubes, and jars — enhancing shelf presence and brand recognition.

Decorative Labels

Premium finishes that elevate brand perception and create tactile, visually rich packaging experiences.

Reclosure labels

Reclosure labels provide a secure seal that can be opened and closed multiple times, ensuring product freshness,protection, and user convenience.

Both Side Printing Labels

Maximize communication space with high-quality reverse side printing for promotions, usage info, or ingredient details.

Peel & Reveal / Multi-Layer Labels

For additional product info, multilingual text, or regulatory content in compact packaging.

Wash Off labels

Wash-off labels are designed to be easily removed with water, allowing a clean separation from containers for reuse or recycling without leaving any residue.

No look / clear-on-clear labels

Provide a minimalist, premium glass like appearance for ultramodern bottle designs.
